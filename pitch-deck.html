AI / Technical Execution — 25%

The AI does real work, not decoration

Forecasting

Every medicine, bed, and footfall forecast is computed from real consumption/occupancy data with a confidence score and named contributing factors — never a bare number.

Redistribution Engine

A haversine-distance surplus search ranks candidate donor facilities and computes an exact transfer quantity + weighted confidence score — a genuine optimization, not a lookup table.

Gemini 2.5 Flash — Explanation Only

A tool-calling agent answers free-form questions grounded in live district data. It explains forecasts and recommendations; it never invents the numbers behind them.

End-to-End & Live

FastAPI backend, Next.js frontend, deployed and reachable today at swasthyagrid.vercel.app — not a slide, a running product.
