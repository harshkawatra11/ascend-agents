03 The Solution

An operations control room, not another dashboard.

Four principles separate a decision-support system from a reporting tool.

01
Predictive
Forecasts shortages, bed occupancy, and patient demand before they occur — not after.
02
Prescriptive
Recommends concrete transfers, staffing moves and redirects — quantities, not vibes.
03
Explainable
Every number ships with a confidence score and the factors behind it. No black box.
04
Human-Governed
AI proposes; an administrator approves, rejects or modifies. Nothing runs on its own.
The difference between “AI + a dashboard” and a system a public-health office can actually run.
